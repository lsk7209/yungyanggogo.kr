# Cup-soup preparation contract research — 2026-09-13

## Decision

`phase6t-cup-soup-*` must not be merged wholesale into `cup-soup-sugar-basis`, and the generated Phase 6T prose is not publishable. A narrower replacement contract is **accepted for further research only**: explain how to read a powdered cup-soup label when the package preparation uses water but the reader adds or substitutes milk.

No title, draft, publication approval or ranking claim is created by this checkpoint.

## Reader decision

The reader needs to decide which numbers come from the packaged powder and which must be added from the actual milk quantity used. The answer must distinguish:

1. water-only preparation, where water adds volume but no macronutrients;
2. water plus a measured amount of milk;
3. full milk substitution;
4. products whose powder already contains milk-derived ingredients; and
5. cases where the package already provides prepared-state nutrition.

This is narrower than the legacy `cup-soup-sugar-basis`, whose generated contract broadly compares sugar, powder soup, milk preparation and sodium without an explicit ingredient-led recalculation method.

## Current evidence

- The MFDS `한눈에 보는 영양표시 가이드라인` was updated in December 2025 and is the current official reference surface for how packaged-food nutrition is displayed and interpreted: https://www.mfds.go.kr/law/board/boardDetail.do?brdId=data0011&menuKey=29&seq=15805
- Food Safety Korea exposes the same December 2025 guideline from its nutrition-label guidance area: https://www.foodsafetykorea.go.kr/portal/board/boardDetail.do?bbs_no=bbs425&menu_grp=MENU_NEW03&menu_no=3719&ntctxt_no=1084998
- A current retail product page for Fontana cup soup records a package-style preparation of one 20 g sachet plus 140 mL hot water and separately describes milk as an optional addition. This supports the existence of a real water-versus-added-milk reading problem, but the retailer page is secondary evidence and its product images/label must be rechecked before drafting: https://www.kurly.com/goods/5067231

## Calculation boundary

- Never treat the powder label as if it automatically includes optional milk.
- Never infer a milk amount from taste instructions such as “취향에 따라”.
- A worked example requires the exact powder serving, exact water/milk recipe, the current powder label, and the current milk label or official food-composition record.
- Added-milk nutrients may be combined only by transparent quantity scaling. Product formulation, milk type and label rounding remain limitations.
- Do not make disease-management, treatment, individualized sodium/sugar limits or “healthier” product claims.

## Source plan required before drafting

1. Current product-label images for at least two domestically sold powdered cup soups with explicit water preparation.
2. At least one product or manufacturer instruction that explicitly permits adding or substituting milk and states a measurable quantity; an unmeasured taste suggestion is insufficient for a numeric example.
3. The December 2025 MFDS nutrition-label guideline sections relevant to serving amount, packaged versus prepared state and additional ingredients.
4. A current official or label-backed milk nutrition source for each worked example.
5. Article-specific capture dates and a calculation worksheet with units and rounding shown.
